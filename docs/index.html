  1. 01

    Multi-tick prediction

    Language models predict the next token. MTP-1302 predicts the next tick: a 32,768 Hz quartz crystal, halved fifteen times, yields exactly one per second.

    32,768 Hz
  2. 02

    Three-year context window

    About 94,608,000 seconds of uninterrupted context on a single SR626SW cell. No summarising. No forgetting. No subscription.

    ~3 yrs
  3. 03

    Low drift

    Drifts less in a month than most models do in a conversation.

    ±20 s / mo
  4. 04

    Depth-aligned

    Water resistant to fifty metres. Survives rain, washing up and the occasional swim. Cannot be jailbroken by a puddle.

    50 m
  5. 05

    Luminous inference

    Glow-in-the-dark hands. Inference continues with the lights off.

    3 heads

    MTP-1302: Multi-Tick Prediction Is All You Need

    The MTP Team*

    Abstract. We present MTP-1302, a three-head analog model for temporal inference. Unlike prior approaches, MTP-1302 is trained on zero tokens, requires zero GPUs and achieves state-of-the-art results on WristBench with a drift of ±20 seconds per month. We find that scaling laws do not apply: the model is, and will remain, 38.5 mm. We release all weights (105 g).

    * Equal contribution, mostly from the battery.
